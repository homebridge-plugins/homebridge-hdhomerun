# Change Log

All notable changes to `@homebridge-plugins/homebridge-hdhomerun` will be documented in this file.

## v1.0.0 (Pending Release)

### Added

- First release, from @Wazza151's HDHomeRun Monitor plugin
- A TV In Use switch, or a switch per tuner, with delays so channel surfing is ignored
- A Poor Reception sensor, and an optional Online sensor, per HDHomeRun
- Estimated watts and kWh on TV In Use over Matter, or everything over Matter only
- Channels DVR recordings and online channels
- A live dashboard in the Homebridge UI and at port 8588
